## Tosox — Mag Retention Reload

The co-op retention implemented here (clear the landing address in the reload's prefix, stow the old
magazine in its postfix, forward the stow result's events, and gate the retaining decision with a small
network packet) follows the architecture of **Mag Retention Reload** by Tosox:

- GitHub: https://github.com/Tosox/SPT-MagRetentionReload

This mod is an independent implementation written for Kirisame Mag Reload; no source code was copied.
